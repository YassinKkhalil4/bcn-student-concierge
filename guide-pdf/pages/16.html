Office hours aren't your hours

Most offices serve the public mornings only, and many close entirely in August and around public holidays. Check the specific office's hours the day before.

A 09:30 appointment is not a 09:30 arrival. Arrive early.

Catalan and Spanish

Both are official. Municipal websites often default to Catalan; look for the language switch, usually top right. The Spanish version is occasionally less complete.

Last-minute check

  • Originals and paper copies
  • 790 form paid and stamped
  • Appointment confirmation + expediente number
  • Names match on every document
