Proof, paperwork and the date you'll forget

Other evidence that's accepted

  • Escritura: the deed, if you own
  • Nota simple from the property register
  • An IBI (property tax) receipt
  • A bank transfer from the last two months showing rent paid

Not accepted: mobile and internet bills, or a utility bill in someone else's name.

Volant or certificat?

Students bring the wrong one, constantly.

Volant

Informative. Fast, often instant online.

Certificat

Carries fe pública: the authenticated document. Separate request, takes longer.

Procedures that need proof ask for the certificat. Check your appointment's own checklist. Unsure? Take the certificat: it covers anything the volant would.

From booking to renewal

OAC = the town hall's citizen office
Book in week one

OAC appointment (cita prèvia). Shorter queue than immigration, not instant.

Register

At the counter, or online with idCAT or Cl@ve.

~7 days later

Confirmation email; volants now available.

Every 2 years

Non-EU Renew, or it lapses silently. Calendar it today.

Your address needs floor and door: ✕Carrer X 44 ✓Carrer X 44, 3r 2a
Never register at an address you don't live at

Barcelona has recorded falsified rental contracts and utility bills submitted for padrón registration. Article 392 of the Criminal Code carries prison sentences and fines for falsifying a document, and it attaches to your immigration file, following you through every renewal you ever apply for. It's not a shortcut.

Catalan vs Spanish: volant and certificat are the Catalan spellings of volante and certificado. Same documents.
